Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how CalcVerse ("we", "our", "the site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit support@calcverse.example.com's website. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

We do not require accounts, logins, or personal information to use our calculators. However, some information may be collected automatically:

  • Usage data: pages visited, time on page, referring URL, approximate geographic region (country level).
  • Device data: browser type, operating system, screen size, language.
  • Log data: IP address, request timestamps, HTTP status codes.

This data is collected by our hosting provider and analytics services for security and performance monitoring.

Calculator Inputs

All calculator inputs are processed locally in your browser. We never transmit, store, or see the numbers you enter into any calculator.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember user preferences (such as unit settings).
  • Measure site usage through analytics.
  • Serve relevant advertisements through third-party advertising partners.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. Some functionality may be affected.

Advertising

We may display advertisements served by third-party ad networks. These partners may use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your browser or the ad network's settings.

Analytics

We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate site usage. Analytics data is anonymized and cannot be used to identify you personally.

Third-Party Services

Our site may embed or link to third-party services (for example, hosting or CDN providers). These services have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

Data Retention

We retain log data for a limited period for security and operational purposes. Analytics data is retained in aggregate form.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example, GDPR in the EU or CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information. To exercise these rights, contact us at support@calcverse.example.com.

Security

We use HTTPS and standard security practices. However, no website can guarantee absolute security — please avoid transmitting sensitive information through any public channel.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@calcverse.example.com.
