Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. They allow websites to remember information between visits — for example, user preferences or session state.

How We Use Cookies

CalcVerse may use the following categories of cookies:

  • Essential cookies: required for the site to function correctly (for example, remembering your unit preferences).
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand aggregate usage so we can improve the site.
  • Advertising cookies: served by third-party ad partners to display relevant advertisements.

Third-Party Cookies

Advertising and analytics partners may set their own cookies. Their use is governed by their own privacy policies.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Instructions for popular browsers:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Preferences → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Disabling cookies may affect functionality.

Do Not Track

We honor standard browser signals where technically feasible. Some third-party services may not.
