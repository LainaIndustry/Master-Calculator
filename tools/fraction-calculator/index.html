What Is a Fraction Calculator?

A fraction calculator performs arithmetic on fractions while keeping them in exact fraction form. Unlike a decimal calculator, it avoids rounding errors — 1/3 stays 1/3 instead of becoming 0.333333...

How to Use It

  1. Enter the numerator and denominator of the first fraction.
  2. Choose the operation (+, −, ×, ÷).
  3. Enter the second fraction.
  4. Results update live.

Formulas

Addition:       a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd
Subtraction:    a/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd
Multiplication: a/b × c/d = ac / bd
Division:       a/b ÷ c/d = ad / bc

After computing, we simplify by dividing numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor (GCD).

Worked Example

2/3 + 1/4:

  • Common denominator: 12
  • 2/3 = 8/12; 1/4 = 3/12
  • 8/12 + 3/12 = 11/12 (already simplified)

Understanding Your Result

The main result is the simplified fraction. The detail box shows the unsimplified form and the decimal equivalent for convenience.

Common Mistakes

  • Adding numerators and denominators directly (2/3 + 1/4 ≠ 3/7).
  • Forgetting to simplify — the unsimplified form is correct but less useful.
  • Dividing by zero — the calculator will show "Undefined".
  • Mixing up reciprocals for division: a/b ÷ c/d = a/b × d/c.
