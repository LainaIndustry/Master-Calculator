What Is a Salary Calculator?

It converts pay between time periods — from hourly to annual, monthly to weekly, and so on. Useful for comparing job offers, freelancing rates, and budgeting.

How to Use It

  1. Enter the amount.
  2. Choose the period ("per hour", "per year", etc.).
  3. Adjust hours/week and weeks/year if needed.
  4. All other periods update automatically.

Formulas

Annual   = Hourly × Hours/Week × Weeks/Year
Monthly  = Annual ÷ 12
Bi-weekly = Annual ÷ 26
Weekly   = Annual ÷ Weeks/Year
Daily    = Hourly × 8 (standard)

Worked Example

$25/hour, 40 hours/week, 52 weeks/year:

  • Daily: $200
  • Weekly: $1,000
  • Bi-weekly: $2,000
  • Monthly: $4,333.33
  • Annual: $52,000

Understanding Your Result

The result is your gross annual pay. Net (take-home) pay will be lower after taxes and deductions.

Common Mistakes

  • Using 50 weeks/year for salaried employees (they're typically paid for 52).
  • Forgetting that salaried employees usually work more than 40 hours.
  • Confusing "bi-weekly" (every 2 weeks, 26/year) with "semi-monthly" (twice a month, 24/year).
  • Comparing gross salaries without considering cost-of-living differences.
