What Is a Tip Calculator?

It computes the appropriate tip on a restaurant bill based on the percentage you choose, adds it to the bill, and (optionally) splits the total across multiple people.

How to Use It

  1. Enter the bill amount (pre-tip total as printed).
  2. Choose a tip percentage.
  3. (Optional) Enter the sales tax rate if you want to tip on the pre-tax subtotal.
  4. (Optional) Enter number of people to split.
  5. Results update live.

Formula

Subtotal = Bill − Tax
Tip = Subtotal × (Tip% ÷ 100)
Total = Bill + Tip
Per Person = Total ÷ People

Worked Example

$84.50 bill, 8% tax, 18% tip, split 4 ways:

  • Tax = 84.50 × 0.08 = $6.76
  • Subtotal = $77.74
  • Tip = 77.74 × 0.18 = $13.99
  • Total = $98.49
  • Per person = $24.62

Understanding Your Result

The main result is the total you'll pay. The detail box shows the tip, tax, and per-person amount.

Standard Tip Percentages

ServiceTypical tip
Restaurant (US)15–20%
Delivery10–15%
Bartender$1–2 per drink
Taxi/rideshare10–15%
Hotel housekeeping$2–5 per night

Tipping customs vary by country. In much of Europe and Asia, tipping is optional or included.

Common Mistakes

  • Tipping on the total after tax instead of the pre-tax subtotal.
  • Forgetting to add the tip when computing the per-person split.
  • Assuming tipping customs are universal — they're not.

Frequently Asked Questions

Pre-tax is considered correct etiquette. Post-tax is also common and slightly more generous.

In the US, 18–20% has become more common for table service. 15% is now considered on the low end.

Many restaurants add 18% for parties of 6+. Check your bill before tipping extra.
