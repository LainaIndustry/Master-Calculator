What Is a Weight Converter?

It converts between units of mass. In everyday use, "weight" and "mass" are used interchangeably — technically, mass is invariant while weight depends on gravity, but for practical purposes (cooking, shipping, fitness) they're treated as equivalent.

How to Use It

Common Mistakes

  • Confusing US tons (2,000 lb) with UK "long" tons (2,240 lb).
  • Confusing ounces (weight) with fluid ounces (volume) — different dimensions entirely.
  • Using lb = 0.45 as an approximation when accuracy matters.
  • Ignoring significant figures — 1 lb ≈ 0.453592 kg exactly.
