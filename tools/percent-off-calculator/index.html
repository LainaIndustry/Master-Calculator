What Is a Percent Off Calculator?

It computes the final price after a percentage discount, plus any sales tax you want to include. Retailers quote discounts as percentages ("25% off"), but the actual savings in dollars depends on the original price.

How to Use It

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount as a percentage.
  3. Optionally add your local sales tax.
  4. Results update instantly.

Formula

Savings = Original Price × (Discount ÷ 100)
Subtotal = Original Price − Savings
Tax = Subtotal × (Tax Rate ÷ 100)
Final = Subtotal + Tax

Example

A $79.99 item at 25% off with 8% tax:

  • Savings = 79.99 × 0.25 = $20.00
  • Subtotal = $59.99
  • Tax = 59.99 × 0.08 = $4.80
  • Final = $64.79

Understanding Your Result

The detail box shows each step of the calculation so you can see exactly where the money goes. The main number is the final price you'll pay at checkout (assuming the tax rate matches).

Common Mistakes

  • Applying the discount after tax — the discount applies to the pre-tax price.
  • Stacking discounts by adding percentages. "20% + 10%" off is not 30% off; it's 1 − (0.8 × 0.9) = 28% off.
  • Confusing "percent off" with "percent of." 25% off $80 = $60; 25% of $80 = $20.
