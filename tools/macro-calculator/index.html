What Are Macros?

"Macros" (macronutrients) are the three main categories of nutrients that provide calories:

  • Protein — 4 cal/g. Builds and preserves muscle, keeps you full.
  • Carbohydrates — 4 cal/g. Main fuel for exercise.
  • Fat — 9 cal/g. Hormones, cell membranes, absorption of vitamins.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your daily calorie target (from our TDEE Calculator, adjusted for goal).
  2. Pick a diet style (balanced, low-carb, etc.).
  3. Optionally enter body weight to see protein per kg.

Formula

Protein (g) = (calories × protein%) ÷ 4
Carbs (g)   = (calories × carb%) ÷ 4
Fat (g)     = (calories × fat%) ÷ 9

Worked Example

2000 calories, balanced (30/40/30):

  • Protein = 2000 × 0.30 ÷ 4 = 150 g
  • Carbs = 2000 × 0.40 ÷ 4 = 200 g
  • Fat = 2000 × 0.30 ÷ 9 = 67 g

Common Mistakes

  • Not hitting protein minimums — the most important macro for body composition.
  • Cutting fat too low (< 0.5 g/kg) — can impair hormones.
  • Chasing "perfect" ratios when total calories matter more.
  • Ignoring food quality — 200 g carbs from soda ≠ 200 g from oats.
