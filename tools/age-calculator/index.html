What Does an Age Calculator Do?

An age calculator determines the exact time elapsed between two dates — usually your date of birth and today. It breaks the difference down into years, months, and days, which is more useful than simply reporting "years old" because it accounts for partial years and leap days.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Leave as of as today, or enter a different date.
  3. Click Calculate. Your exact age and totals appear instantly.

How Age Is Calculated

The most common method is the "YMD" (Years, Months, Days) approach. The algorithm:

  1. Subtract the birth year from the target year.
  2. Subtract the birth month from the target month; if negative, subtract 1 from the year count and add 12.
  3. Subtract the birth day from the target day; if negative, borrow days from the previous month (using its actual length).

This avoids the errors of just dividing days by 365.25, which doesn't account for varying month lengths.

Example

If you were born on 15 March 1990 and today is 20 September 2025:

  • Years: 2025 − 1990 = 35
  • Months: 9 − 3 = 6
  • Days: 20 − 15 = 5
  • Result: 35 years, 6 months, 5 days

Understanding Your Result

You'll see your age in YMD form plus cumulative totals: total months, total weeks, total days, total hours, and total minutes. The "next birthday" line tells you how many days remain until your next birthday from the reference date.

Common Mistakes

  • Dividing total days by 365 and rounding — this ignores leap years.
  • Forgetting time zone when comparing exact instants (usually irrelevant for age).
  • Assuming month length is always 30 days — it isn't.
