What Is an Auto Loan Calculator?

An auto loan calculator computes the monthly payment on a car loan. Because car purchases usually involve a down payment, trade-in, and sales tax, this calculator accounts for all three before computing the financed amount.

How to Use It

  1. Enter the vehicle price.
  2. Add your local sales tax rate.
  3. Subtract trade-in value and down payment.
  4. Enter the interest rate (APR) and term.
  5. Click Calculate.

Formula

Amount Financed = Price + Tax − Trade-In − Down Payment
M = P × [ r(1+r)^n ] ÷ [ (1+r)^n − 1 ]

Where r is the monthly rate (APR ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of months.

Worked Example

A $30,000 car with 7% tax, $5,000 trade-in, $3,000 down, at 6.5% for 5 years:

  • Tax: $2,100
  • Amount financed: 30,000 + 2,100 − 5,000 − 3,000 = $24,100
  • M ≈ $471.55/month
  • Total paid ≈ $28,293; interest ≈ $4,193

Understanding Your Result

The result is your monthly payment on principal and interest only. Dealerships often quote a payment that also includes taxes, title, registration, and GAP insurance — get the full breakdown in writing.

Common Mistakes

  • Forgetting sales tax when comparing quotes — it varies widely by state.
  • Focusing on monthly payment instead of total cost. A 72-month loan lowers the monthly but raises total interest substantially.
  • Ignoring the trade-in's impact on tax — in many states, trade-in reduces the taxable amount.
  • Not accounting for dealer fees and add-ons (documentation, extended warranty).
