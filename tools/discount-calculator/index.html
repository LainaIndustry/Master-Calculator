What Is a Discount Calculator?

It computes the final price after a percentage discount and shows how much you save.

How to Use It

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Results appear instantly.

Formula

Savings = Price × (Discount ÷ 100)
Final price = Price − Savings

Worked Example

A $99.99 item at 25% off:

  • Savings: 99.99 × 0.25 = $25.00
  • Final: $74.99

Understanding Your Result

The main result is the price you'll pay after the discount. The detail box shows the original price, discount percent, and dollar savings.

Common Mistakes

  • Adding stacked percentages. "20% + 10%" is 28% off, not 30%.
  • Confusing "20% off" with "20% of." 20% off $100 = $80; 20% of $100 = $20.
  • Forgetting that sales tax is applied after the discount (see our Percent Off Calculator).
