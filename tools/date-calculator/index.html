What Does a Date Calculator Do?

It performs calendar arithmetic — adding or subtracting a duration from a date and returning the resulting day, including its weekday. Common uses include scheduling, deadlines, loan terms, pregnancy due-date estimation (informal), and contract periods.

How to Use It

  1. Pick a starting date (today is prefilled).
  2. Choose add or subtract.
  3. Enter the amount and select the unit.
  4. Results update immediately.

Formula

Date arithmetic depends on the unit:

  • Days: add the number of days directly.
  • Weeks: multiply weeks by 7, then add days.
  • Months: increment/decrement the month field, clamping the day if it exceeds the target month's length.
  • Years: increment/decrement the year (Feb 29 handling: clamps to Feb 28 in non-leap years).

Example

Starting from 2025-03-15, add 90 days → 2025-06-13 (a Friday).

Understanding Your Result

The main result is the target date in long form. The detail box shows the change applied and total days difference, useful when mixing units (e.g. 1 month + 15 days).

Common Mistakes

  • Assuming every month has 30 days.
  • Forgetting leap years (Feb 29 exists every 4 years, except centuries not divisible by 400).
  • Mixing up "add 1 month" with "add 30 days" — they're different.
