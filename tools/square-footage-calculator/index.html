What Is Square Footage?

Square footage is the area of a space measured in feet on each side. It's the standard unit for real estate, flooring, painting, landscaping, and roofing in the US.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter the length and width in any supported unit.
  2. (Optional) Enter a price per square foot to estimate material cost.
  3. Results update as you type.

Formula

Area = Length × Width

Convert units first if needed:

  • 1 ft = 12 in = 0.3048 m
  • 1 yd = 3 ft = 0.9144 m
  • 1 m² = 10.7639 ft²

Worked Example

A room 12 ft × 10 ft:

  • Area = 120 sq ft
  • In square meters: 120 × 0.092903 ≈ 11.15 m²
  • If flooring costs $4.50/sq ft: $540

Understanding Your Result

The result is net area. Add 5–10% for waste (cuts, offcuts) when ordering materials. For odd-shaped rooms, split into rectangles and add each area.

Common Mistakes

  • Mixing units — inches and feet in the same calculation.
  • Forgetting to add waste when ordering materials.
  • Using this for L-shaped rooms without splitting into rectangles.
  • Confusing linear feet (length) with square feet (area).

Frequently Asked Questions
