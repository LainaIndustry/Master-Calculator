About Date & Time Calculators

Date math looks simple until you deal with leap years, varying month lengths, time zones, and daylight saving transitions. Our tools handle all of this correctly using the standard YMD algorithm and UTC-safe arithmetic.

What You'll Find Here

  • Age Calculator: exact age in years, months, days, plus total weeks and hours.
  • Date Calculator: add or subtract days, weeks, months, or years from any date.
  • Time Duration: hours and minutes between two times, including overnight.
  • Hours / Time Card: work hours with break deductions.
  • Day Counter / Day of Week: days between dates and weekday lookup.
  • GPA & Grade: weighted and unweighted grade calculations.

How Date Math Works

To compute age or date difference, we use the YMD (Years, Months, Days) algorithm:

  1. Subtract years. If the month/day hasn't occurred yet, subtract one.
  2. Subtract months. If the day hasn't occurred yet, borrow days from the previous month (using its actual length).
  3. Subtract days with the borrow applied.

This is more accurate than dividing total days by 365.25, which ignores variable month lengths.

Time Zones

Our date tools operate on the dates you provide as calendar dates, not instants. Time zone conversion is a separate concern — see the Time Zone Calculator for reference conversions.
