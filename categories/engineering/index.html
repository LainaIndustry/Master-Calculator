About Engineering Calculators

Our engineering tools cover the most common calculations on construction sites, in labs, and in workshops. Each tool includes the formula used and its assumptions.

What You'll Find Here

  • Construction: concrete, gravel, mulch, tile, roofing, square footage, stairs.
  • Electrical: Ohm's law, voltage drop, resistor color code, electricity cost, BTU.
  • Mechanical: horsepower, engine HP, tire size, fuel cost.
  • Chemical: density, molarity, molecular weight.
  • Networking: IP subnet, bandwidth.

Key Engineering Formulas

  • Ohm's law: V = I × R
  • Power: P = V × I
  • Concrete volume: length × width × depth (in consistent units)
  • BTU: ~20 BTU per square foot for heating; ~25 BTU for cooling
  • Horsepower: HP = (Torque × RPM) ÷ 5252

Important Disclaimer

Verify before building. Construction and electrical calculations must comply with local building codes and standards. Our results are estimates — always confirm with a licensed professional and check against applicable codes (NEC, IBC, etc.).
