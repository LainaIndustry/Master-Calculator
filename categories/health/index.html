About Health & Fitness Calculators

These tools help you understand your body composition, energy needs, and fitness progress. They're based on published formulas widely used in clinical and sports-science settings — but they're estimates, not medical diagnoses.

What You'll Find Here

  • Body composition: BMI, body fat percentage (Navy and Army methods), lean body mass, body surface area.
  • Energy: BMR (basal metabolic rate), TDEE (total daily energy expenditure), calories burned, calorie needs.
  • Nutrition: macronutrient splits, protein, carbohydrate, and fat intake.
  • Weight goals: ideal weight, healthy weight range, body type.
  • Fitness: pace calculator, target heart rate, one-rep max.
  • Lifestyle: sleep cycle calculator.

How These Calculators Work

Most health calculators use well-established formulas:

  • BMI: weight (kg) ÷ height (m)²
  • BMR (Mifflin-St Jeor): 10×weight + 6.25×height − 5×age + s (s = +5 men, −161 women)
  • TDEE: BMR × activity multiplier (1.2 to 1.9)
  • Body fat (Navy): uses logarithms of neck, waist, and hip circumference

Each tool page documents the exact formula and its limitations.

Important Health Disclaimer

Not medical advice. Our health calculators provide general informational estimates. They do not diagnose, treat, or replace consultation with a licensed healthcare professional. Always consult your doctor or a registered dietitian before making significant health, diet, or exercise changes.
