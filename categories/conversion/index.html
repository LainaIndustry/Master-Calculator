About Unit Converters

Unit conversion is a common task in science, engineering, cooking, travel, and everyday life. Our converters use exact or near-exact conversion factors recognized by international standards (NIST for imperial↔metric, SI definitions for metric prefixes).

What You'll Find Here

  • Length: meters, centimeters, kilometers, inches, feet, yards, miles, nautical miles.
  • Weight & mass: grams, kilograms, ounces, pounds, stones, tonnes.
  • Temperature: Celsius, Fahrenheit, Kelvin — with correct offset arithmetic.
  • Area: m², ft², acres, hectares, in².
  • Volume: liters, milliliters, gallons (US and UK), cups, pints, fluid ounces.
  • Speed: km/h, mph, m/s, knots, feet per second.
  • Time: seconds, minutes, hours, days, weeks, months, years.
  • Special: shoe size converter, Roman numeral converter.

How Unit Conversion Works

Every converter works the same way:

  1. Convert the input to a base unit (e.g. meters for length).
  2. Convert from the base unit to the target unit.
  3. Apply rounding appropriate to the input precision.

Temperature is an exception — it uses offset arithmetic (C = (F − 32) × 5/9), not just multiplication.
