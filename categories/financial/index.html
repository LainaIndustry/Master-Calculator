How Financial Calculations Work

Most financial calculations use one of a handful of formulas:

  • Amortized loan payment: M = P × [ r(1+r)ⁿ ] ÷ [ (1+r)ⁿ − 1 ]
  • Compound interest: A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n·t)
  • Future value of annuity: FV = PMT × [ (1+i)ᵐ − 1 ] ÷ i
  • Present value: PV = FV ÷ (1 + r)ⁿ
  • ROI: (Gain − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100

Every tool page documents the exact formula used and includes a worked example.

Important Note on Accuracy

Our results are estimates based on the values you enter. Real-world outcomes depend on lender fees, taxes, market returns, inflation, and other factors we can't model generically. Always verify critical financial decisions with a licensed advisor, accountant, or lender.
